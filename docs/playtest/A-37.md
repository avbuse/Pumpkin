# A-37 playtest: redstone dust power

Use a creative player in a new flat overworld. The signal strength is the dust's `power` property, which is also how bright the dust looks. Alternate Current stays off. The experimental redstone datapack stays off. Comparators, repeater delay, observers, and dispensers are later tasks.

1. Place a redstone block, then a straight line of 16 dust on stone, running east.
   Expect the first dust (next to the block) to be fully lit and each further dust to be one step dimmer. Count the lit dust: 15 are lit, and the 16th is dark. `/data get block` on the first dust reports `power` 15, the 15th reports `power` 1, and the 16th reports `power` 0.

2. From the second dust in that line, branch one dust north and one dust south.
   Expect the junction to stay one step below the first dust, and both arms and the dust just east of the junction to be one step below the junction. The arms match each other. Power is not split in half.

3. One block east of a powered dust, place a stone with dust on top of it. Leave air above the lower dust.
   Expect the upper dust to be one step dimmer than the lower dust.
   Put stone in the air above the lower dust.
   Expect the upper dust to go dark and stay dark.

4. Place dust on top of a stone, a redstone block next to that dust, and one dust on the ground beside the stone, with air between the upper dust and the column the lower dust sits in.
   Expect the lower dust to be one step dimmer than the upper dust.
   Fill the gap with stone so the lower dust has stone above it and stone beside it.
   Expect the lower dust to go dark.

5. Place stone between a redstone block and a dust, so the dust does not touch the block.
   Expect the dust to stay dark. The block only weakly powers the stone.

6. Point a repeater west into a stone block and power the repeater from the east with a redstone block. Set the repeater to 1 tick. Place dust on the west side of the stone.
   Expect the dust to become fully lit (`power` 15) when the repeater powers, not one step dimmer. The wait is the repeater's delay. The dust itself does not add another delay. Do not fail this step because the repeater is one game tick late. That miss is the scheduler note in `TestPlan.md`, owned by A-38.

7. Power a line of four dust from a redstone block, then break the block.
   Expect every dust in the line to go dark in the same moment. None of them should stick at 1.

8. Power three dust from both ends with a redstone block on each end.
   Expect both end dust to be fully lit and the middle dust to be one step dimmer (`power` 14). It takes the stronger end, not the sum.

9. Break the second dust of a line powered from the west.
   Expect the dust still touching the block to stay fully lit, the broken cell to be air, and the dust east of the gap to go dark.

Capture: a recording of steps 1, 2, and 3, close enough that the brightness steps and the blocked climb are visible.
