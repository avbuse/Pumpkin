# A-16 playtest: sleep in a bed and set the spawn

Use a survival player in a new overworld. Death and the respawn screen are A-27. This checklist checks that a bed click stores a spawn when the bed is in range and clear above, including when sleep itself is refused.

1. Day, clear weather, standing next to an unoccupied bed. Right-click it.
   Expect the action-bar line that you can only sleep at night, and a chat line that the respawn point was set. You stay standing. The bed is not occupied.

2. At night, summon a zombie a few blocks from a second bed's head so its body is inside 8 blocks horizontally and 5 blocks vertically of that head (`/summon minecraft:zombie`). Right-click that bed.
   Expect the monsters-nearby action-bar line and a chat line that the respawn point was set. You do not lie down. The bed stays unused.

3. Move or kill that zombie. At night, with no monster that prevents rest near the second bed, right-click it again.
   Expect to lie down and the bed to look occupied. Chat does not repeat the respawn-point line, because step 2 already stored this bed.

4. Leave the bed (sneak, or wait until morning).
   Expect to stand up beside the bed. The bed is free again.

5. At night, summon a slime, magma cube, ghast, phantom, or shulker beside a bed and right-click it.
   Expect to lie down. Those mobs do not block sleep. A calm zombified piglin also lets you lie down. Anger that piglin at yourself and right-click again: expect the monsters-nearby line, and you stay standing.

6. Switch to creative (`/gamemode creative`) with a zombie still beside a bed, at night. Right-click the bed.
   Expect to lie down. Creative skips the monster check.

7. Stand several blocks away from a bed that also has a solid block on top of it, and right-click.
   Expect the too-far action-bar line. The spawn does not move. Standing next to that same blocked bed expects the obstructed line, and the spawn still does not move.

8. In the Nether or the End, right-click a bed.
   Expect the bed to explode. It does not become your spawn.
