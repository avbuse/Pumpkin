# A-16 playtest: sleep in a bed and set the spawn

Use a survival player in a new overworld. Death and the respawn screen are A-27. This checklist only checks that a bed click stores a spawn when sleep starts, and that a refused click does not.

1. Day, clear weather, standing next to an unoccupied bed. Right-click it.
   Expect the action-bar line that you can only sleep at night. You stay standing. The bed is not occupied. Chat does not say that the respawn point was set.

2. Run `/time set night`. Move any hostile mobs well away from the bed (more than 8 blocks horizontally and 5 blocks vertically from the head). Right-click the bed.
   Expect to lie down, the bed to look occupied, and chat to say the respawn point was set.

3. Leave the bed (sneak, or wait until morning).
   Expect to stand up beside the bed. The bed is free again. Right-clicking that same bed the next night should not repeat the respawn-point chat line.

4. At night, summon a zombie a few blocks from the bed head so its body is inside 8 blocks horizontally and 5 blocks vertically of the head (`/summon minecraft:zombie`). Right-click the bed.
   Expect the monsters-nearby action-bar line. You do not lie down. The bed stays unused. Chat does not say the respawn point was set. The bed from step 2 is still the stored spawn.

5. Switch to creative (`/gamemode creative`) with that zombie still beside the bed, at night. Right-click the bed.
   Expect to lie down. Creative skips the monster check.

6. In the Nether or the End, right-click a bed.
   Expect the bed to explode. It does not become your spawn.
