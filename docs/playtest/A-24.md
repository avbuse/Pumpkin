# A-24 Melee damage and attack cooldown

Record one continuous session. Survival, no Strength or Weakness, difficulty does not matter. Use a wooden sword unless a step says otherwise.

1. Full-charge hit.
   Do: `/gamemode survival`, `/give @s wooden_sword`, `/summon zombie ~ ~1 ~ {Health:20f,NoAI:1b}`. Wait until the attack bar is full, then hit the zombie once.
   Expect: one strong hit sound. `/data get entity @e[type=zombie,limit=1,sort=nearest] Health` reports 16. You take no damage.

2. Spam click.
   Do: with the same sword, hit the zombie again immediately, then keep clicking until the bar is only partly full and the zombie can be hurt again.
   Expect: the click inside the hurt flash plays the no-damage sound and does not change Health. The next hit, before the bar fills, reduces Health by less than 4.

3. Critical hit.
   Do: wait for a full bar, jump, and hit the zombie while falling. Do not sprint.
   Expect: the critical-hit sound and particles. Health drops by 6, not 4.

4. Sweep.
   Do: `/summon zombie ~1 ~1 ~ {Health:20f,NoAI:1b}` so two zombies are within a block of each other. Stand on the ground, do not sprint, wait for a full bar, and hit one with the wooden sword.
   Expect: the sweep sound and the sweep particle. The other zombie loses 1 health. A marker armor stand in that same space is not hit: `/summon armor_stand ~ ~1 ~ {Marker:1b,Health:20f}` stays at 20. You take no damage.

5. Sprint knockback.
   Do: sprint into a zombie and hit it with a full bar.
   Expect: the zombie is knocked back farther than a standing hit. You hear the knockback sound and the strong-hit sound, and you stop sprinting.

6. Armoured target.
   Do: `/summon zombie ~ ~1 ~ {Health:20f,NoAI:1b,equipment:{chest:{id:"minecraft:leather_chestplate",count:1}}}`. The zombie must be wearing the chest. `ArmorItems` is not read on 26.3. Full bar, standing hit, wooden sword.
   Expect: Health is about 16.16, not 16. The leather chest is still equipped.

7. Sharpness.
   Do: `/give @s wooden_sword[enchantments={sharpness:1}]`. The tooltip must show Sharpness I. If it does not, hold a wooden sword and run `/enchant @s sharpness 1`. Full bar, standing hit on a fresh 20-health zombie. Then a falling critical hit on another fresh zombie.
   Expect: the standing hit drops Health from 20 to 15. The critical hit drops it from 20 to 13, and you see both the critical and magic-critical particles.

8. Sweeping Edge III.
   Do: hold a wooden sword and `/enchant @s sweeping_edge 3`. The tooltip must show Sweeping Edge III. Two fresh `NoAI` zombies within a block of each other, both at 20. Stand on the ground, do not sprint, full bar, hit one.
   Expect: the second zombie loses 4 health, so Health is 16. That is `1 + (3/4) * 4`. Sweeping Edge I on the same hit loses 3 (Health 17). Sweeping Edge II loses about 3.67 (Health about 16.33). Switching off the sword and sweeping again loses only 1.

9. Item swap.
   Do: fill the attack bar, switch to another hotbar slot, and switch back.
   Expect: the bar empties and fills again.

10. Creative.
    Do: `/gamemode creative`. Full-charge wooden sword hit on a 20-health zombie.
    Expect: the zombie takes damage and you take none. Set survival again when you finish.
