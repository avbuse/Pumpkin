# A-13 playtest: axe strip, shovel path, and hoe till

Use a survival player in a new overworld. Each step starts with the tool in the main hand and the target block within reach. Durability is the damage number on the tool.

1. Right-click an oak log with an axe. Repeat on oak wood, and on a log whose axis is sideways.
   Expect a stripped oak log or stripped oak wood. The sideways log stays sideways. The axe takes 1 durability. You hear the strip sound.

2. Right-click exposed copper, oxidized copper, and a waxed copper block with an axe.
   Expect exposed copper to become a copper block, oxidized copper to become weathered copper (one stage, not all the way back), and the waxed block to become an unwaxed copper block of the same stage. Scrape and wax-off use their own sounds and particles. Each use costs 1 durability.

3. Right-click one half of a copper door and one half of a double oxidized copper chest that has items inside.
   Expect both door halves to change together and keep facing, open, and hinge. Expect both chest halves to drop one oxidation stage together. The items are still in the chest.

4. Right-click a grass block with a shovel, with air, cave air, or void air above it.
   Expect a dirt path, the flatten sound, and 1 durability. The same click on the bottom face does nothing. A grass block with stone, water, or a flower above it stays grass.

5. Right-click dirt and grass with a hoe, with air above, from the top or a side.
   Expect farmland with moisture 0, the till sound, and 1 durability. The bottom face does nothing. Dirt with a solid block or water above it stays dirt. Coarse dirt with air above becomes dirt, not farmland. Rooted dirt becomes dirt from any face, including underneath, and drops hanging roots even when a block is above it.

6. Hold a stick and right-click an oak log, a grass block, and dirt.
   Expect no change and no durability, because a stick is not a tool.

7. Switch to adventure (`/gamemode adventure`) and right-click an oak log, grass, and dirt with an axe, shovel, and hoe.
   Expect no strip, path, or farmland. Switch back is not required for the next step.

8. In adventure, right-click a lit campfire with a shovel.
   Expect the campfire to go out. That click is the campfire's own action, so adventure still allows it.

9. In creative, right-click an oak log with an axe.
   Expect the log to strip and the axe to keep full durability.
