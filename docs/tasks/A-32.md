## Goal

Right-clicking a villager opens the trade window from the loaded trade data. Completing one offer takes the inputs and gives the output. A villager with no offers opens an empty trade list.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/villager/ or the existing villager file`
- `the trade screen handler`

## Acceptance criteria

- The window opens.
- One emerald-for-item offer, if the vanilla trade table contains one at novice level, completes once.
- The inputs are consumed.

## Verification

Harness window clicks.

## Out of scope

- Restocks, gossip, and workstation pathfinding.
