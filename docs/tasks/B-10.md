## Goal

Noise fill should write palette indices directly and recompute the section heightmap once at the end of the section. Skip per-block neighbour updates during generation. Mismatch counts must stay inside the A-45 baseline.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-world/src/generation/ (proto chunk fill)`

## Acceptance criteria

- A generated section's block states match the previous filler on a fixed seed and chunk.
- The baseline document is updated with the new timings, not with worse mismatch counts.

## Verification

The proto chunk test and a timed note.
