## Goal

Chunks whose light neighbourhoods do not overlap can be lit on the rayon pool. Overlapping chunks stay serial. Results match serial lighting.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-world/src/lighting/`

## Acceptance criteria

- Two distant chunks lit together match two chunks lit one after another.
- Two adjacent chunks are not lit at the same time.

## Verification

A lighting test with two fixtures.

## Out of scope

- Changing light values.
