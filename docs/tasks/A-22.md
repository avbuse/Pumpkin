## Goal

Wheat, or the crop block that already has a growth property, advances a stage on random tick when light and farmland match vanilla. Bone meal advances it by the vanilla random amount and is consumed. A fully grown crop is not consumed.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/bone_meal.rs`
- `the wheat or crop block file under crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/`

## Acceptance criteria

- Bone meal on age 0 wheat increases the age and consumes one bone meal.
- Bone meal on a fully grown crop does not consume the item.
- A random tick can increase age when the vanilla conditions hold.

## Verification

Gametest that forces a random tick or calls the growth function once.

## Out of scope

- Every crop. One crop plus a shared helper is enough if the others use the same property.
- Villager farming.
