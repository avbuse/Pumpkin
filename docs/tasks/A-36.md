## Goal

Add a pumpkin-gametest that builds a few small redstone layouts and records the update order and the final power. The first version may fail on layouts Pumpkin gets wrong. Mark those cases with the expected vanilla result and leave them ignored until A-37, A-38, and A-39 fix them. The fixture's job is to name the expected order, not to change the evaluator.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-gametest/src/block_based/`

## Acceptance criteria

- The fixture builds dust, a repeater, and a piston from block states.
- At least one layout that already matches vanilla passes.
- Layouts that do not match are ignored with a one-line reason, not deleted.

## Verification

cargo test -p pumpkin-gametest for the new module.

## Out of scope

- Changing redstone_wire.rs or piston.rs. Later tasks own those.

## Rules

Read `docs/tasks/README.md` before starting. One task on one branch. Commit with `git commit -s`. Push only to `avbuse/Pumpkin`. Do not open a pull request and do not push to `Pumpkin-MC/Pumpkin`. Do not commit a Mojang jar, region file, or decompiled source, and do not paste Java into the repo. Class names in the research notes are pointers. Reimplement from the description. Clippy denies `unwrap`, `expect`, `panic`, and printing to stdout.
