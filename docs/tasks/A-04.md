## Goal

A fresh 26.3 world stores DataVersion 5023. Pumpkin rejects anything above 4903, so that world never opens. Raise the accepted maximum to 5023 and write new chunks with 5023. Keep the existing minimum. This is the version gate only. Do not build a general DataFixer, and do not rewrite chunk NBT.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-world/src/world_info/mod.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-world/src/chunk/format/anvil.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A synthetic level.dat whose DataVersion is 5023 passes the version check.
- 4435 still passes and 4434 still fails.
- 5024 fails.
- Chunks Pumpkin writes advertise 5023.
- Comments name 5023 as the 26.3 data version.

## Verification

A unit test that builds the version check inputs without a full world. Do not commit a Mojang level.dat.

## Out of scope

- Chunk field changes between 4903 and 5023. A-08 records those, A-48 applies them.
- Restoring time and weather. That is A-05.
- Any historical upgrader before 4435.
