## Goal

Repeated blasts in the same blocks recompute the same exposure rays. Cache the exposure between an explosion centre and an entity for the duration of that explosion. Damage and broken blocks match the uncached blast on the A-30 fixture.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/world/explosion.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- The A-30 creeper fixture still breaks the same blocks.
- Exposure is computed once per entity per blast.

## Verification

The A-30 gametest.

## Out of scope

- Changing explosion power.
