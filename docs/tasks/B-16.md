## Goal

Chunk packets are bulk. Keep-alive, movement corrections, and block updates are urgent. Give each connection two outbound queues and drain urgent first. Also cap concurrent logins that have not finished configuration. Gameplay packets are unchanged.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/net/java/`

## Acceptance criteria

- An urgent packet queued after a large chunk packet is still written before the rest of the bulk queue.
- Login attempts beyond the cap wait or disconnect with a clear reason.
- pumpkin_smoke still reaches play.

## Verification

A unit test of the queue order plus pumpkin_smoke.

## Out of scope

- Anti-xray. B-27.
- Compression backends. B-06.
