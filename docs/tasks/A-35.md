## Goal

A warden that has reached the vanilla anger threshold and has a target uses the sonic boom instead of only melee. The boom damages through a wall the way vanilla does, at the vanilla range. Below that anger it still melee-attacks.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/mob/warden.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- At the boom anger threshold, a target in range takes sonic-boom damage.
- A solid wall between them does not cancel that damage.
- Below the threshold, the boom does not fire.

## Verification

Gametest that sets anger and ticks the warden.

## Out of scope

- Darkness and the emerge animation, which already exist.
- The sculk shrieker spawning path.
