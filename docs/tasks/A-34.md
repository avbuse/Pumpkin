## Goal

A breeze that can see a player throws a wind charge on its attack interval using the existing wind-charge entity. The charge's existing impact behaviour stays as it is.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/mob/breeze.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A breeze with a target spawns one wind charge toward that target.
- A breeze with no line of sight does not spawn one.

## Verification

Gametest that ticks the breeze attack goal.

## Out of scope

- Jump and slide behaviour beyond the throw.
