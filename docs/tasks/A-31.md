## Goal

Feeding two adult animals of one species their breeding item puts them in love and produces a baby. Killing one adult drops the vanilla food item for that mob when the loot table loads. Pick one species and finish it.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/passive/ (one species)`
- `the breed goal under entity/ai/goal/`

## Acceptance criteria

- Two fed adults spawn one baby.
- The breeding items are consumed.
- An adult drops the expected food item on death.

## Verification

Gametest or harness summon plus use-item.

## Out of scope

- Every species.
- Breeding cooldowns beyond the one vanilla timer this species uses.
