## Goal

A hopper above a container pulls one item on the vanilla cooldown, and a hopper pointing into a container pushes one item. A powered hopper does not transfer. This is the cooldown and the single-item move, not the sleep optimisation.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/hopper.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/entities/hopper.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- One item moves from a chest above a hopper into the hopper.
- One item moves from a hopper into a chest it points at.
- A powered hopper moves nothing.
- The cooldown matches the vanilla hopper interval.

## Verification

Gametest with a chest, a hopper, and a few ticks.

## Out of scope

- Block-entity sleep. B-05.
- Minecart hoppers.
