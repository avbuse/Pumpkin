## Goal

Holding use on a food item consumes it when hunger is below full, or when the food is always edible, and adds the food and saturation values from the item data. A full hunger bar rejects ordinary food.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/hunger.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/ (the food use path that already exists)`

## Acceptance criteria

- Eating one bread, or the closest food the item data already describes, raises food level by that item's nutrition.
- Eating at full hunger with ordinary food does not consume the item.
- The stack shrinks by one.

## Verification

Harness: give bread, set hunger below full, use the item, read the food level.

## Out of scope

- Potion effects from food.
- Combat. A-24 owns attack cooldown in living.rs.
