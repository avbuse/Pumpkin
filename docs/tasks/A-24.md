## Goal

A player's melee hit applies damage from the held item, the attack cooldown, and the target's armor points that already exist. A full-cooldown hit with a wooden sword deals that sword's damage. A spam click deals the reduced vanilla fraction. Creative players still do not take damage.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/player.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/swords.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/living.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A full-charge hit reduces a test mob's health by the sword's attack damage, minus armor if the mob has any.
- An immediate second hit deals less damage.
- The attacker takes no damage from their own swing.

## Verification

Harness or gametest with a summoned mob and a known health value. Compare the health delta with the item's attack damage attribute.

## Out of scope

- Bows. A-25.
- Shields. A-26.
- Mob AI attacks. A-28 and later.
- Hunger exhaustion from combat, unless it is one call into the existing hunger manager.
