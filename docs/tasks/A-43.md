## Goal

EnterBlock is an empty match arm. Fire it when the player enters a block. Fire the location and inventory triggers when the matching predicate from A-42 passes. Existing triggers keep their current payloads.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/player/advancement/trigger.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Walking into a block of the type named by an advancement grants that criterion.
- An inventory criterion that checks for one item grants when that item is obtained.
- A location criterion grants at the matching position.
- A criterion whose predicate fails does not grant.

## Verification

Unit test or harness with one advancement JSON.

## Out of scope

- The whole story tab. A-44.
- Every vanilla trigger type.
