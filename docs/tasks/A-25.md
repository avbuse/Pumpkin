## Goal

Drawing a bow with an arrow and releasing spawns an arrow whose velocity follows the draw time. The arrow damages a mob it hits, using the damage path from A-24. An empty inventory does not fire in survival.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/bow.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/projectile/ (arrow)`

## Acceptance criteria

- A full draw spawns one arrow and consumes one arrow in survival.
- A short draw spawns a slower arrow.
- The arrow reduces a mob's health on impact.

## Verification

Harness: give a bow and arrows, release a full draw toward a summoned mob, observe health.

## Out of scope

- Crossbows and tridents.
- Infinity and Flame.
