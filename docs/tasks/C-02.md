## Goal

A player can request a teleport to another player, and the target accepts or denies. Requests expire. The commands are unregistered when the flag is off.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/command/commands/tpa.rs`
- `commands/mod.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Flag off: unregistered.
- Accept moves the requester to the target.
- Deny and expiry do not move anyone.
