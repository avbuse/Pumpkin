## Goal

A brewing stand with water bottles, nether wart, and blaze powder completes the vanilla brew time and produces awkward potions. Fuel is consumed. Missing fuel or ingredient leaves the bottles as water.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/entities/ (brewing stand)`
- `the brewing stand block file`

## Acceptance criteria

- Water plus nether wart plus blaze powder yields awkward potions.
- No blaze powder means no progress.
- The brew time matches the vanilla stand.

## Verification

Gametest that ticks the stand for the recipe duration.

## Out of scope

- The full potion tree.
- Splash and lingering recipes.
