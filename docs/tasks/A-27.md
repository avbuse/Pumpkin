## Goal

At zero health the player dies, drops their inventory under the vanilla keep-inventory rule, and can respawn. The respawn position is the bed from A-16 when it is still valid, otherwise the world spawn. Items on the ground are real item entities.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/player.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Lethal damage sets the player into the death state and then allows respawn.
- With keepInventory false, the items are on the ground and the new inventory is empty.
- With keepInventory true, the items remain.
- A valid bed respawn uses that bed.

## Verification

Harness: apply damage until death, respawn, and check the spawn position and inventory.

## Out of scope

- XP orbs beyond what the existing drop path already does.
- Scoreboard death counters.
