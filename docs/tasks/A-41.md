## Goal

An observer emits a one-tick pulse when the block it faces changes. A dispenser with a water bucket places water when powered and does nothing when empty. Other dispenser items can stay on the existing path.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/redstone/observer.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/redstone/dispenser.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A block change in front of an observer powers the output for one redstone tick.
- A powered dispenser with a water bucket places a source.
- An empty dispenser does not drop a ghost item.

## Verification

Gametest.

## Out of scope

- Dropper item selection beyond one bucket.
- Every dispenser projectile.
