## Goal

A sticky piston pulls the attached block on retract when vanilla would. A piston updates from quasi-connectivity when the block above it becomes powered. Add those two cases to the fixture.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/piston/piston.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Sticky retract brings one slime-attached stone back.
- Powering the block above a piston extends it.
- A normal piston from A-39 still pushes.

## Verification

Gametest cases added beside the A-36 fixture.

## Out of scope

- Zero-tick and locational update-order puzzles beyond these two cases.
