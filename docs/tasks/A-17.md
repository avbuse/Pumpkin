## Goal

A furnace with one smeltable input and fuel finishes one output using the datapack recipe timing. Fuel is consumed according to the recipe's fuel time. An empty fuel slot does not smelt.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/furnace.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/entities/ (furnace entity)`

## Acceptance criteria

- Raw iron plus coal produces one iron ingot and consumes the fuel.
- No fuel means the input stays.
- The lit block state follows whether smelting is in progress.

## Verification

Gametest or harness: place a furnace, insert the two stacks, tick until the recipe time has elapsed, read the output slot.

## Out of scope

- Blast furnace and smoker, unless they share the same function and the change is one match arm.
- Hopper insertion. A-21.
