## Goal

When enabled, ores in a chunk sent to a client are hidden until the client has a line of sight, using the engine mode the admin picked. Default off sends the real blocks. This changes the client's view of hidden blocks only. The server's world is unchanged.

## Files and crates

- `chunk packet encoding under crates/pumpkin-core/src/net/ or pumpkin-world`
- `config`

## Acceptance criteria

- Flag off: the chunk packet contains the real ore.
- Flag on: an unexposed ore is replaced in the packet and the stored chunk still has the ore.
- A neighbouring air block exposes the ore on the next update.

## Verification

A unit test of the packet rewrite. Do not require a client mod.

## Out of scope

- Changing world generation.
