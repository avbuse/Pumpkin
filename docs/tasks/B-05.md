## Goal

Hoppers with nothing to move still tick. Skip the transfer work when the hopper, the source, and the destination have not changed since the last attempt, and wake on inventory change, redstone change, or neighbour block change. A hopper that can move an item must not stay asleep.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/entities/hopper.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/hopper.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- An empty hopper pointing at an empty chest does not move items and does not scan inventories every tick.
- Inserting an item wakes it and the item moves on the next cooldown.
- A powered hopper stays idle.
- The A-21 transfer tests still pass.

## Verification

Gametest plus a counter of transfer attempts. Remove the counter before committing, or keep it behind a test cfg.

## Out of scope

- Sleep for every block entity type.
- Entity activation range.
