## Goal

A piston pushed by a short redstone pulse extends and moves one pushable block. It does not move bedrock or an over-limit line. A sticky piston's basic retract is A-40. This task is the normal piston push.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/piston/piston.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- One stone block moves when a normal piston extends.
- Bedrock does not move.
- A line longer than the vanilla push limit does not move.
- The matching A-36 case passes.

## Verification

The gametest fixture.

## Out of scope

- Sticky retract and quasi-connectivity. A-40.

Cut `core/A-39-piston` from `core-functionality`. Read `docs/tasks/README.md` and `docs/BRANCHING.md`. Merge the task branch back into `core-functionality` only. Commit with `git commit -s`.
