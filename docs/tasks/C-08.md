## Goal

A player can claim one cuboid. Other players cannot break or place inside it. The owner and operators can. Flag off means no claims are checked. Claims persist.

## Files and crates

- `a claim command and a check in the existing break and place paths`

## Acceptance criteria

- Flag off: a second player can break a block.
- Flag on: that break is rejected inside the cuboid and allowed outside it.
- The claim survives a restart.

## Verification

Harness place and dig.

## Out of scope

- Claim groups, trusts, and multiple claims.
