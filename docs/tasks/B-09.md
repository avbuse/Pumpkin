## Goal

Cap how many chunk loads and generates one player can have in flight, and delay unloading a chunk that was in view a moment ago. Defaults should be high enough that the compat smoke test's view distance still receives its chunk. Document the numbers next to the config fields.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-world/src/chunk_system/`
- `pumpkin config for the new caps`

## Acceptance criteria

- A player at view distance 2 still receives the chunk the smoke test expects.
- A chunk that left view is not discarded on the same tick.
- The caps are config fields with defaults.

## Verification

pumpkin_smoke plus a unit test of the cap counter.

## Out of scope

- Changing simulation distance rules.
