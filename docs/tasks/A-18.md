## Goal

The 2x2 inventory grid and the 3x3 crafting table should resolve one shaped recipe and one shapeless recipe from the loaded datapack, place the result, and consume the inputs when the result is taken. Special recipes such as banners and fireworks stay out of this task.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/data/datapack/ (recipe lookup only if the API is missing a match function)`
- `the crafting screen handler under crates/pumpkin-core or pumpkin-inventory`

## Acceptance criteria

- Planks in the shaped pattern for a crafting table produce a crafting table.
- A shapeless recipe already present in the vanilla data, such as a dye mix the pack defines, produces its result.
- Taking the result consumes the matching inputs and leaves the other slots.

## Verification

Harness window clicks once A-03 exists, or a unit test over the recipe matcher with datapack JSON already in assets/.

## Out of scope

- Stonecutter, loom, and smithing.
- Every special remainder recipe.
