## Goal

Worn armor reduces incoming melee damage by the vanilla armor formula for the pieces equipped. A raised shield blocks a frontal melee hit and takes durability. A hit from outside the block arc is not blocked.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/shield.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/player.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/living.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Leather chestplate reduces a known hit by the vanilla armor amount for that piece.
- A raised shield facing the attacker blocks one frontal hit.
- A hit from behind is not blocked.

## Verification

Gametest or harness with two controlled health values.

## Out of scope

- Trim, protection enchantments, and the full armor stand of pieces beyond one chestplate plus the formula.
