## Goal

A repeater delays the signal by its setting and acts as a diode. A comparator in compare mode outputs the rear signal when it is greater than or equal to the side signal, and subtract mode outputs the difference. Unignore the matching A-36 cases.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/redstone/repeater.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/block/blocks/redstone/comparator.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A 4-tick repeater delays by four redstone ticks.
- A signal does not pass backwards through a repeater.
- Compare and subtract each match one fixture.

## Verification

The A-36 gametest, with those cases enabled.
