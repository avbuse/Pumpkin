## Goal

An operator sets a named warp. Players teleport to it when the flag is on. Warps persist. Flag off unregisters the commands.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/command/commands/warp.rs`
- `commands/mod.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Flag off: unregistered.
- A warp survives a restart.
- A player teleports to the saved position.

## Verification

Console sequence.

## Out of scope

- Per-warp permissions beyond operator-set and anyone-use.
