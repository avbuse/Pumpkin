## Goal

A zombie in range of a survival player swings on the vanilla attack interval and applies its attack damage through the same damage path as A-24. It does not swing through a wall.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/mob/zombie.rs`
- `the melee attack goal it already uses`

## Acceptance criteria

- A zombie next to a player reduces that player's health after the attack cooldown.
- A zombie with a solid block between it and the player does not land the hit.

## Verification

Gametest with a spawned zombie and a fake or real player. If the harness cannot hold a player still, a unit test of the goal's reach check plus one damage call is the stand-in, and say so.

## Out of scope

- Reinforcements, door breaking, and drowned conversion.
