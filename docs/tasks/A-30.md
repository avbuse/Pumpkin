## Goal

A primed creeper explodes after its fuse, breaks blocks in the vanilla survival pattern for that power, and damages entities in range. A creeper that leaves range and loses its target stops when vanilla would. Do not change the explosion ray cache. That is B-23.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/entity/mob/creeper.rs`
- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/world/explosion.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- A primed creeper removes a known block inside the blast and leaves a known block outside it.
- An entity inside the radius takes damage.
- The fuse time matches the vanilla creeper.

## Verification

Gametest in a flat stone box.

## Out of scope

- Charged creepers.
- Mob griefing gamerule if it already gates explosions. Honour it if the flag exists.
