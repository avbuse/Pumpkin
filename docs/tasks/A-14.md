## Goal

An empty bucket picks up a source block of water or lava and becomes a filled bucket. A filled bucket places a source in an empty space and becomes empty. Respect the existing fluid code. Do not invent a second fluid simulator.

## Files and crates

- `crates/pumpkin-core/src/item/items/bucket.rs`

## Acceptance criteria

- Bucket on a water source yields a water bucket and removes the source.
- Water bucket on a replaceable block places a source and leaves an empty bucket.
- The same pair of cases works for lava.
- A bucket used on a flowing block does not delete a source that vanilla would keep.

## Verification

Harness or gametest for the four cases above.

## Out of scope

- Milk, fish, and powder-snow buckets.
- Cauldron interactions.
